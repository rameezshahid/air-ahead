About the AQHI

The Air Quality Health Index rates the health risk from air pollution on a scale from 1 to 10+. It combines ground-level ozone, fine particulate matter and nitrogen dioxide; during wildfire smoke events, Environment Canada calculates it from fine particulate matter alone. Quebec uses its own Info-Smog program, so its communities aren't listed.
